# Auditing the branch from raw material, not summaries

**The deeper version of "status of research/payoff-geometry-v1-1" is an evidence audit, not a longer status report.** The branch's headline status (CI green, watches green, 89 ahead and 101 behind main) rests on four lossy layers: a report's daily series, one primary book compounded, one text line, then a three-line window in SESSION_STATE.md. A top professional would ignore those digests and start from per-run report artifacts, the exchange data behind them, and git and Actions metadata. They would first ask what each watch can decide at all, because at 16 days (and even at 180) a daily Sharpe test can only catch breakage, not confirm a Sharpe near 1. They would treat the roughly 15 watches as one correlated family, reconcile forward P&L against independent prices and funding, and measure the open `_xs_weights` next-day-open look-ahead instead of calling it "slightly flattering". The repo has strong selection-bias machinery already (trial ledger, BH, null-consistent deflated Sharpe, single-look guard, drawdown envelopes). What is missing is mostly power statements, provenance, independent reconciliation and a measured look-ahead. The rewritten prompt at the end asks for exactly that, with pass/fail criteria committed before any look.

## How to read the evidence labels

Three tiers are used throughout. **Verified from repo files** means a researcher read the file (they had no shell, so no git history was inspected and the local clone is shallow). **Researcher arithmetic** means a calculation derived from a formula; formulas are shown. **Unverified** means a code reading not yet confirmed by a test, a formula recalled from a paper and flagged "verify", or a library whose maintenance and licence were not checked. Web fetches of several sources failed, so some literature claims rest on abstracts or search snippets.

Two internal tensions matter before anything else. First, the dashboard and the fast-gates calibration still carry superseded numbers: `scripts/dashboard_snapshot.py` hard-codes the 2.09 / 4.48 blend and 1.39 / 3.16 untouched rows as "pass" with notes, and `config/fast_gates_v1.json` calibrates the G2 alarm on the 1.39 Sharpe, while the repo's own later tests give about 1.05 (Binance), 1.09 (Hyperliquid) and 0.67 (untouched coins with MEXC funding) (verified from repo files). ## 1. What a top professional would do that you did not ask for

**Decide what each watch can decide before reading its P&L.** With iid returns, Var(SR_hat) is about (1 + SR^2/2)/T with SR and T in matching annual units (researcher arithmetic, formula from memory of Bailey and López de Prado, verify) ([Risk.net](https://www.risk.net/journal-risk/2223785/sharpe-ratio-efficient-frontier)). To reject SR = 0 at one-sided 95% (z = 1.645) when the true SR is 1 takes T about 1.645^2 x 1.5 / 1 = **4.1 years**; at SR 0.5, about 12 years. After 16 days the SE of annualized Sharpe is sqrt(365/16), about **4.8**, so a realized Sharpe of +1 and of -3 are both consistent with a true +1. The minimum detectable true Sharpe at 5% one-sided size and 80% power is about 2.49/sqrt(T years): **3.5 at 180 days, 2.5 at 365** (iid; fat tails and autocorrelation push it higher). The repo's own `forward_formal_reviews_v1.json` note puts the 180-day figure near 2.8, so the two derivations differ by assumption and should be reconciled. Either way, the 180-day reviews are breakage gates, not edge tests. No MinTRL, power or futility statement was found in the files searched (confirm before acting).

**Treat the watches as one family.** About 15 concurrent watches are about 15 comparisons, and many share legs and BTC beta (blend, trend core, vol-target, cross-asset). A professional pre-declares one primary watch, reports the rest as a family (BH is already in `review_methods.bh_family` but scoped per registration), and estimates effective N from the eigenvalues of the daily watch-return correlation matrix. Harvey, Liu and Zhu argue for a t above 3.0 against a 316-factor zoo ([NBER w20592](https://nber.org/papers/w20592)). The repo's trial count of 11 covers only 9 plus 2 ledgered strategy-search trials; it excludes zoo families, prop-firm runs, leverage-bracket grids (72 and 288 cells), sentiment and Kronos work (verified from repo files). It is a floor, not the search size.

**Attribute and reconcile, do not just compound.** Decompose forward P&L into BTC beta, price, funding, fees and spread, with a residual, and read realized cumulative P&L and drawdown against the existing bootstrap envelope percentiles. Being down 2 to 9% at 16 days in a falling BTC tape is expected to be mostly beta; test that rather than assume it. Implementation fidelity (signal, position, price, funding, P&L parity) is decidable in days, unlike Sharpe. Perold-style implementation shortfall applies to the daily rebalance of a 70-coin book. These are practitioner inferences; no authoritative web source for a named reconciliation standard was found.

**Audit the process itself.** McLean and Pontiff found out-of-sample decay of 26% and post-publication decay of 58% across 97 predictors ([Ivey copy](https://ivey.uwo.ca/media/3775549/pontiff.pdf)), and Hou, Xue and Zhang found 65% of 452 anomalies fail |t| of 1.96 ([NBER w23394](https://www.nber.org/papers/w23394.pdf)). A decay-adjusted prior for the blend's expected live Sharpe is therefore well under 1. Fade24 is the best of 288 bracket cells, so regression toward zero is built in (SESSION_STATE.md, verified).

## 3. Methods and tools

**Power and decidability.** Compute PSR and MinTRL per watch. Formulas as recalled (verify against the paper): PSR(SR*) = Phi((SR_hat - SR*) sqrt(T-1) / sqrt(1 - g3 SR_hat + ((g4-1)/4) SR_hat^2)), and MinTRL = 1 + (1 - g3 SR_hat + ((g4-1)/4) SR_hat^2) (z / (SR_hat - SR*))^2. Use 365 days for crypto, not 252. The notes suggest a short in-house implementation (about 20 lines) cross-checked against `jsharpe` or `sharpebench`; maintenance of these and `pypbo` is unchecked. Label each watch monitor or test by whether its MDE exceeds any plausible haircut Sharpe (about 0.5 to 1). An optional anytime-valid confidence sequence for early futility is an adaptation, since no source applies it to Sharpe and crypto fat tails strain its assumptions ([Wang and Ramdas](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.03722v3), [Howard et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.06476v5)).

**Family and attribution.** Eigenvalue-based effective N from the watch-return correlation matrix; BH across the family at the repo's q = 0.10; deflated Sharpe with n_trials as the conservative raw count; HAC (Newey-West) regression on BTC via `statsmodels`; separate price, funding, fee and spread ledgers. Bootstrap block length can be checked with `arch.bootstrap.optimal_block_length` against the repo's 20-day mean block (library behavior from general knowledge, unverified).

**Look-ahead.** `SESSION_STATE.md` records that `multi_premia_human_forward._xs_weights` requires the next day's open to mark a coin tradable (verified, lines 41 to 44; the mask is `opens.loc[d].notna() & ret.loc[d].notna()` with `ret = opens.shift(-1)/opens - 1`). The researchers' code reading is that `xs_premia.run_xs` (lines 53 to 54) uses the identical mask, so the blend's S2 and S3 legs, the development script and, via inheritance, the paper account share it. **A second reading of `xs_premia.py` (lines 52 to 54) confirms the mask is identical: `tradable = opens.loc[d].notna() & ret.loc[d].notna()`. No test confirms its reach, and no file measures its size.** The method is a paired replay: run the unmodified function and a variant that does not condition on d+1, on the same frozen inputs, and report the difference in Sharpe, daily returns and turnover, plus a unit test with a coin that delists the next day. Pair it with the existing truncation-invariance check, and first confirm that `test_truncation_invariance.py` covers `run_xs` (not verified). A planted one-bar look-ahead should be flagged by the detector as a positive control.

**Reconciliation and controls.** Binance public data gives daily and monthly zips with sha256 checksums ([binance-public-data](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data)); Hyperliquid `fundingHistory` gives hourly funding (from SDK and third-party guides, not the official docs) ([SDK method](https://app.unpkg.com/@nktkas/hyperliquid@0.33.1/files/esm/api/info/_methods/fundingHistory.js)); Coin Metrics community data is non-commercial with thin sub-daily history ([docs](https://docs.coinmetrics.io/packages/coin-metrics-community-data)). No free MEXC archive was confirmed. G1 (paper vs V_ALL, correlation 0.9992, tracking error 0.34%/yr) is self-consistency on the same data, not independent. Other controls: a planted-signal harness with a null by permutation, a direct MEXC vs Binance basis decomposition to test the funding-artifact mechanism (labelled "unverified" in the repo), and a known-answer check on CTREND against the paper with a pre-committed band (headline paper numbers were not retrieved, so no target is quoted) ([Cambridge](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/trend-factor-for-the-cross-section-of-cryptocurrency-returns/4C1509ACBA33D5DCAF0AC24379148178)).
