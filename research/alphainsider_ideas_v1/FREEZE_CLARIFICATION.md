# AlphaInsider-inspired scan v1 — pre-result implementation clarification

Frozen before any local strategy PnL is calculated.

- U.S. regular trading hours are 09:30-16:00 America/New_York, resolved with DST for each date. The FREEZE.json `rth_utc` pair is a summer-DST shorthand, not a fixed UTC session across January-September.
- The S9 and S6 `signal_window` values 14:00:00-17:00:00 remain UTC exactly as frozen. A candidate must also fall inside that date's actual U.S. regular session. This clarification does not move those signal windows.
- Massive 5-minute bars are treated as bars labeled by their opening timestamp. Any required signal, entry, exit, lookback, initial-balance, prior-close, or touch-detection bar missing at its exact timestamp makes that observation ineligible. No interpolation or substitute bar is allowed.
- Gross return is `direction * (exit_open / entry_open - 1) * 10000` bps. Net return subtracts the frozen round-trip all-in friction once. The fully reversed control uses the same timestamps with `direction=-original_direction` and pays the same friction.
- S10: prior regular-session close is the close of the exact 15:55 ET bar. Today's gap uses the exact 09:30 ET open. Entry is exact 09:35 ET open. For a small-gap trade, touch detection begins with the 09:35 bar and ends with the 10:25 bar inclusive; prior close is touched when `low <= prior_close <= high`. Exit is the next exact 5-minute open after the first touch. If no touch occurs in that search interval, exit is exact 10:30 ET open. Large-gap trades exit exact 10:30 ET open.
- S9: cumulative RTH VWAP is `sum(bar_vwap*bar_volume)/sum(bar_volume)` from exact 09:30 ET through the signal bar inclusive. The 30-minute price and VWAP comparisons use the exact bar 30 minutes earlier; all intervening 5-minute bars from the RTH open through the signal are required. A candidate requires state and pullback on the same signal bar. Signals are accepted by deterministic chronological greedy scheduling per ticker: accept the earliest eligible candidate when flat, enter the next exact 5-minute open, exit at the exact open 30 minutes after entry, and ignore candidates whose signal occurs while the prior position is still open.
- S6: the initial balance requires exactly the six bars opening 09:30, 09:35, 09:40, 09:45, 09:50 and 09:55 ET. IB high/low are the extrema across those six bars. The first eligible close outside the IB within the frozen UTC signal window is the only trade for that ticker-session. Entry is the next exact 5-minute open and exit is the exact open 60 minutes after entry.
- Positive-PnL concentration is the largest positive instrument total divided by the sum of all positive instrument totals. If there is no positive instrument contribution, the concentration gate fails.
- No family, threshold, instrument universe, time window, friction level, or direction may be changed after local results are viewed under this protocol ID.
