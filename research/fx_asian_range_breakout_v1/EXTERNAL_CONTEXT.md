# External context — FX Asian-range breakout v1

This note is contextual only and was added after the strategy protocol was frozen. It does not change thresholds, hours, markets, costs, or gates.

- BIS research describes London and New York as the main centers of FX activity and notes that liquidity is typically high around the London open and during the London/New York overlap.
  - https://www.bis.org/publications/qr-201312/anatomy-global-fx-market-through-lens-2013-triennial-survey
  - https://www.bis.org/publ/work1094.pdf
- Ito & Hashimoto document strong intraday seasonality in FX activity and volatility using EBS data, including elevated activity around major regional market openings.
  - https://www.nber.org/papers/w12413
  - https://www.nber.org/papers/w10856

These sources motivate testing session structure. They do not validate the frozen Asian-range breakout rule or imply positive expectancy.
