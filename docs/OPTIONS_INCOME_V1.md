# Cash-secured option put spreads v1

New strategy family: receive option insurance premiums, with a purchased lower-strike put protecting the downside. No moving averages, trend signals, funding arbitrage or gamma-exposure indicator. No Bollinger features. Existing research and prospective protocols are unchanged.

This replaces the initial unregistered condor idea before any option-price economics: call-side delivery fees can grow with settlement price. Puts allow a rigorous total-loss bound including settlement fees. This is a new own adaptation, with no prior option-price outcome inspected or parameters fitted. The public instrument catalog was checked for availability only.

BTC and ETH linear USDC options only. Each sleeve uses the earliest expiry7–14 days away, sells the highest put strike <=90% of current index and buys the highest put strike <=80%, same expiry. It holds to expiry. Executable bid/ask depth is used, never a theoretical mark fill. Separate REST quotes are indicative, not atomic execution. A5-second maximum age and complete depth are mandatory.

Paper capital10,000USDC, at most2,500 cash reserved and100 conservative maximum loss per sleeve. Reserve full short-put strike times underlying quantity plus long premium, entry fees and worst delivery fees; do not assume margin offsets. Size conservatively from width plus fee bounds, floor to underlying minimum increment, and never upsize based on predicted profits. Account margin, combination-order reliability, venue access and actual account costs remain unverified. No live orders or claimed readiness.

For amount q, strikes Ks>Kl, net executable entry credit c, entry fee f, settlement price S>=0, payoff is `q*(max(Kl-S,0)-max(Ks-S,0))+c-f-delivery(S)`. Standard trading fee per leg is `q*min(.0003*index,.125*executed_unit_premium)`. Delivery per put is `q*min(.00015*S,.125*intrinsic)` on ITM legs only. No daily or combo discounts. Because an ITM put requires S<K, delivery is bounded above by `q*.00015*(Ks+Kl)`. Thus conservative total loss is at most `q*(Ks-Kl)+f+delivery_bound-c`. Stress doubles fees and reduces total credit by10%; sizing reserves that conservative stress bound without relying on the credit. Cash yield zero.

Current [Deribit linear options specification](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/31424932728093-Linear-USDC-Options) specifies ITM physical conversion into the matching future followed immediately by USDC cash settlement, producing the usual intrinsic payoff. [Published fees](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944746248989-Fees) and [margin rules](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944811317149-Margin-types-and-usage) are separate constraints. The API amount is underlying units; contract size must not be multiplied twice. BTC/ETH multiplier1 is verified from metadata.

The first test combines current executable option quotes with terminal return scenarios from the entire existing verified underlying history. Use nonoverlapping blocks of ceil(DTE/8h) bars, beginning at the first retained close. Every historical scenario is spent development evidence. This is not a backtest of historical option prices, implied volatility, spreads or fills, and it is not prospective confirmation. Report mean, loss frequency, worst loss, conditional tail loss and a seeded2000-resample5th percentile of the sample mean for both costs. The screen requires positive credit and positive mean/lower bootstrap percentile under both costs plus all cash/risk/depth/freshness gates. It cannot establish that today's implied-volatility regime predicts future returns. A failed screen remains closed without strike/date/asset retuning.

Freeze this definition before fetching option-price outcomes. Retain raw API replies, hashes, timestamps, metadata and scenario PnL. A survivor requires a separate finite forward protocol before any profitability claim; no phantom historical option PnL, current quotes substituted into past dates, or unapproved broker connection.
