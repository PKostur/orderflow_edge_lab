# Four-regime allocation v1

New fixed multi-asset allocation:25%SPY stocks,25%TLT long Treasuries,25%GLD gold,25%SHV short Treasuries. The economic rationale is growth, deflation, inflation and liquidity diversification. This trades no trend/calendar-return signal and is separate from the rejected equity-month-end timing hypothesis. The published [permanent-portfolio allocation explanation](https://www.aaii.com/journal/article/the-permanent-portfolio-using-allocation-to-build-and-protect-wealth) supplies the allocation rationale. Fixed quarterly full-rotation execution is our adaptation, not a claimed replication of original band rebalancing. Incidental secondary performance snippets were exposed during source search; this is development evidence, not a blind test.

Freeze rules before downloading TLT/GLD or evaluating this portfolio. Existing SPY/SHV source is reused unchanged; those prices have already been inspected in a different rejected hypothesis. TLT launched2002, GLD2004, SHV2007. The fixed2009–2026Sep study window remains unchanged and includes2020/2022; it omits2008 and must not be described as a2008crisis test. Every observation is historical development data.

Initial10,000USD. Sell all held asset quantities at each quarter's last regular planned exchange-session close, then buy at the next actual open. Unexpected closure never backdates a trade; a missed exit rolls forward and reentry waits for exit completion. Equal budgets are fixed from known cash after sale. Each asset's amount is integer shares with a102%previous-close limit-on-open and cost-inclusive sizing. Missing an opening limit leaves that sleeve in cash until next quarterly allocation; no redistribution, retry or fill at marks. Broker settlement/account/auction-depth and actual fees remain unverified. No borrowed money, shorts or live orders.

Full quarterly liquidation/reopening deliberately charges every leg; no favorable continuous-weight rebalancing approximation. Baselinecost per side `max($1,.005*shares)+.00015*notional`; stress doubles all costs. ETF expenses already affect observed market prices. Use raw unadjusted prices and ex-date dividends only once. Receivables count toward NAV, but remain unusable for90calendar days; actual dividend payment dates have not been reconstructed. Cash yields0, and Treasury ETF income comes from observed distributions/price behavior. Currency conversion, taxes, stable broker fees and fill calibration are not implied.

Planning calendar excludes knowledge of all ad-hoc closures; actual availability only matches or postpones orders forward. Archive full actual/planning calendar and source hashes. Splits, missing quotes or incompatible sessions abort rather than repair. No current-month/asset/date substitutions after returns are viewed. Controls (allSHV, allSPY,60/40SPY/TLT, equalweightSPY/TLT/GLD) run the same funded quarter-rotation/dividend/cost engine; their risk differs and all are shown. Controls are not selectable rescue candidates.

Predeclared development screen:>=4%CAGR, CAGR aboveSHV,<=25%close drawdown, all fixed2009–13/2014–18/2019–23/2024–26 blocks positive, no annualreturn below−20%, each at both costs. These are practical historical screening criteria, not proof of an alpha or future profits. Report all actual cash/receivables, share ledgers, costs, years, blocks, drawdowns and controls. A survivor may enter a separately frozen forward paper study; no promotion or live order path is authorized.
