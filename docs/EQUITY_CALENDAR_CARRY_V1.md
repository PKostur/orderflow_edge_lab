# Equity calendar and Treasury carry v1

One new economic family outside crypto: an equity calendar allocation, with Treasury-bill ETF holdings outside the equity window. SPY and BIL are fixed before any price download. No indicators, leverage, shorting, crypto or options. Existing frozen lanes remain unchanged.

McConnell/Xu's [2008 paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=917884) studies the last-session return and first three next-month returns. This own adaptation enters at last-session OPEN and exits third-session CLOSE, avoiding cash-sized orders at an unknown close. It therefore omits the preceding overnight return and is not exact paper replication. A prior unsolicited secondary modern-performance search snippet was exposed; no blind-source claim. No own price outcomes have been viewed before this freeze.

Execution: liquidate BIL at the preceding session close; place a fixed limit-on-open SPY buy at102% of the previous close, integer quantity sized from already-known cash and limit-inclusive costs. Fill only when opening price is strictly below limit; otherwise remain cash for the entire equity window. On the third next-month session, sell held equity at close. Buy BIL next session open with the same limit/cash rule. No same-auction use of unknown sale proceeds, fractional orders, intraday retry or model financing. The holding periods permit sale cash to settle before subsequent exit under historical settlement conventions, but broker account functionality and auction fill/depth parity are unverified. Daily vendor open/close is an explicit research fill assumption.

Primary2009Jan–2026Sep sample is selected by publication date and last completed quarter, not PnL. Download an extra December2008 calendar/price lead-in. Archive and validate the XNYS schedule from exchange_calendars4.11.1, including special closures/early closes. Any missing symbol/session, invalid OHLCV or split blocks evaluation. Historical calendars are known-in-advance schedule assumptions; no dates inferred from future PnL.

Use raw unadjusted daily prices plus dividends; never add dividends to adjusted returns. Ex-date entitlement uses prior-close held quantity, including when sold at the ex-date open. Receivables enter NAV but cannot finance trades until90 calendar days after ex-date. This delayed cash rule is deliberately explicit and is not an exact historical payment-date reconstruction. ETF expenses are embedded in observed prices; do not subtract them again. Cash yields0; BIL distributions and price moves supply carry, not a headline annual-yield proxy. Taxes and currency conversion remain outside the model.

Costs each side: `max($1, $0.005*shares) + .00015*notional`; stress doubles the entire amount. Current [IBKR published US fixed commissions](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/pricing/commissions-stocks.php) motivate the commission component; no user broker, account fee or historical spread is verified. The additional1.5bps is an execution/other-fee assumption, not a calibrated guarantee. Both ETF legs incur costs on every switch. Source prices/fees/issuer returns are not promises of future profit.

Report funded cash, integer inventory, receivables, every fill/unfilled order, dividend, close equity, intraday-low stress bound, CAGR, drawdown, calendar/year/block returns, turnover/costs and exposure. Controls: BIL, SPY, cash, fixed tenth-to-thirteenth session equity window and100 seeded random four-session monthly windows. All use the same wallet/dividend/cost rules. Random controls preserve monthly four-session exposure and replay the whole wallet; do not shuffle trade profits or select the best month phase. Use corrected random tail `(1+controls>=candidate)/101`. Controls are diagnostics, not extra tuned candidates. No retuning after any outcome.

Frozen development screen: positive net return and CAGR above BIL at both costs, drawdown<=25%, positive fixed2009–13/2014–18/2019–23/2024–26 compounded blocks, baseline random-window right tail<=.10. This screen cannot establish a prospective persistent edge. A survivor requires separate future confirmation and execution validation; no live orders or promotion.
