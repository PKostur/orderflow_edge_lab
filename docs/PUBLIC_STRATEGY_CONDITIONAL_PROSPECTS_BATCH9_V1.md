# Conditional prospects and the completion criterion

Status at 2026-10-11: **no viable candidate**. All thirteen fresh historical adaptations tested through batch 9 were rejected. A viable candidate must pass a separately frozen confirmation study on observations collected after its rules are frozen. Engineering checks and positive historical averages do not satisfy that requirement.

These are conditional research hypotheses, not recommendations to trade. There is no evidenced special session, volatility state or asset filter that rescues the rejected versions. The two high-volatility Ichimoku observations are insufficient for a regime claim; even its larger low-volatility group remains spent descriptive evidence. Source searches are bounded and do not establish that all public strategies have been exhausted.

## Future confirmation

A passing historical screen must be followed by an immutable candidate and adequately powered future-only protocol, realistic calibrated execution/cost evidence, controls, complete admissions and failure accounting, and approval-bound paper reliability. Those observations must arrive after the freeze. No historical interval will be relabeled untouched, no thresholds weakened, and no live orders are authorized.

Frozen DON8, EMA8, VOL8, DV2, D4, payoff geometry, session watches and prospective protocols remain preserved. Bollinger Bands are excluded entirely from this new research.
