# Cash trend portfolio v1

One new, own adaptation; no revisions to previously frozen candidates. Rules fixed before running it. No Bollinger features. This is a practical low-turnover portfolio attempt, not a claim of a proven trading edge.

Use only BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT and LINKUSDT Spot, $10,000 shared simulated cash. Three return signs over 90, 270 and 1095 eight-hour bars represent 30/90/365 days. Average all three signs and clip below zero. Each target weight is `0.25 * score * min(1, 0.10 / annual_volatility)`. Annual volatility is sample standard deviation of the last 180 log returns times sqrt(1095). Zero volatility uses factor one. Incomplete history means zero weight; bad prices or missing bars fail the entire input. No redistribution, leverage, shorts, interest or external cash.

AQR's [published trend rationale](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/Journal-Article/AQR-JPM-Fall-2017.pdf) uses 1/3/12-month trends and volatility scaling in diversified long/short futures. Our four-coin long/cash, weekly, 60-day-volatility implementation is our adaptation. Their performance does not establish crypto profitability.

Every Monday00 UTC completed close (Sunday16-labelled bar), fix desired quantities from current close-mark NAV and closes. Execute at Monday08 open, a deliberate eight-hour delay. Sell excess holdings first, deduct costs, then scale all buy deficits pro rata if cash including costs is insufficient. Quantities are fractional theoretical Spot units; no account fee, exchange lot/depth/partial-fill or live execution parity is claimed. Costs are 20bps per side baseline and 40bps stress, covering fees/spread/slippage as a conservative assumption. Actual account economics require later calibration. Target cap25% applies at decision; weights drift between rebalances. No stop or dynamic circuit breaker is implied by that cap.

Arithmetic is Python float64 with `math.fsum` aggregation. No exchange lot rounding is modeled. Cash-limited pro-rata buys leave a relative1e-14 rounding reserve. Negative cash raises; no clipping, borrowing or refund. Candidate mechanics were independently reviewed before market outcomes.

Use the entire existing manifest-verified common panel, August11 2020–September30 2026, warmup1095 bars. It includes the 2022 bear market and is entirely previously inspected development data. No date, asset or parameter optimization. Cash, a same-volatility/same-cap always-long control, and full-capital BTC buy/hold are reported separately. BTC has different risk and is not the primary control. Cash returns0. Costs are charged on every rebalance; final positions are valued net of a hypothetical liquidation cost. Entries/fills are wallet adjustments, not independent closed trades.

The predeclared historical screen requires positive funded net PnL under both costs, <=15% close-mark drawdown, positive incremental net return over the matched always-long control under both costs, and positive compounded return after leaving out each calendar year. These are engineering/development screening rules, not statistical proof. All years and controls must be shown even when failing. One strategy attempt; controls/cost scenarios are not additional tuned candidates. Prior minimum32 trading/33 signal-model cells remain recorded. No rescue tuning.

Historical NAV is marked at every close; a same-bar-low stress bound is also reported, not presented as a realizable simultaneous price path. Buy/hold and control comparisons use the same eligible start/end and costs. Full equity and fill ledgers are saved. Historical survivor status is only eligibility to design a separate prospective confirmation. New future observations and a finite frozen confirmation protocol are required before a profitability claim or promotion. Existing prospective protocols and paper-only/no-order boundaries remain unchanged.
