# Research control plane

The research control plane is a read-only status surface over four already bounded components:

1. the frozen universal session/alignment prospective shadow;
2. the Jev v1 research decision artifact;
3. the bounded Jev research action artifact;
4. the universal shadow operational monitor.

It does not create evidence, alter strategies, change frozen definitions, or transmit orders.

## Stages

- `PRE_START`: the prospective boundary has not been reached.
- `WAITING_FOR_FIRST_POST_START_CHANGE`: the boundary has passed, but no frozen target has changed after the start and no prospective episode exists.
- `WAITING_FOR_COMPLETIONS`: at least one post-start target change or open prospective episode exists, but no completed prospective trade exists yet.
- `ACCUMULATING_COMPLETED_EVIDENCE`: at least one completed prospective trade exists, but the frozen review gate is not met.
- `READY_FOR_REVIEW`: the existing frozen review requirements are met.
- `HALTED_PROTOCOL_BREACH`: Jev policy or deterministic integrity checks have stopped the research pipeline.

## Evidence boundary

Operational target state is shown next to prospective evidence, but it remains explicitly non-evidence. Inherited pre-start positions can explain why the prospective ledger is empty, but their PnL and age do not enter prospective scoring.
