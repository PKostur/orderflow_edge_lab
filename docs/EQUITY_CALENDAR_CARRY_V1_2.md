# Equity calendar carry v1.2: causal closure handling

An independent pre-PnL review caught a real lookahead defect in the actual-calendar prototype: Hurricane Sandy closures would have backdated the October2012 Treasury sale to October26, before the October28 announcement. No economic evaluation had run. The blocked original implementation/review remain in history; this correction is frozen before the first PnL calculation.

Planning uses the regular NYSE schedule without all ad-hoc holidays (Sandy2012Oct29/30, Bush mourning2018Dec5, Carter mourning2025Jan9). Treat all as unknown when planning, even when some were announced earlier. Random monthly controls also use these planned lengths. Actual market closures cancel a scheduled buy or pre-window Treasury sale; do not retroactively shift it earlier. A scheduled equity exit on a closed day is delayed until the next open session close. Buy Treasury at the next actual open after that scheduled/rolled exit. If the previous asset remains held because a sale was canceled, block buying the other asset. There is no post hoc forced sale to rescue a missed equity entry.

This separates historical market-availability matching from the information used to choose orders. The new source bundle retains both actual and regular-planning calendars and the ad-hoc closure list. No strategy price thresholds, monthly window, universe, cost, cash, or screen changes. V1/v1.1/v1.2 are three registrations of one equity-calendar hypothesis, with zero economic runs before v1.2. All data remain retrospective development; no future-proof claim.
