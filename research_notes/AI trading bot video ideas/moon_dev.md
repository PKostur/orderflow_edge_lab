## Q1. Which agents/bots exist, and for each: inputs, decision rule, data feed?

### Takeaway
The 2025 "AI agents" repo is mostly LLM wrappers: the numeric trigger is a hard-coded threshold on a feed (liquidations, OI, funding), and then an LLM is asked to answer BUY/SELL/NOTHING with no tested decision rule behind it. The only agents with a stated, rule-based, data-derived signal are the 2026 Polymarket "5-minute bots", which are built on a liquidation feed and explicitly labelled hypotheses. The repo contains no strategy that is validated by code.

### Inferences
- The 2025 directional agents amount to "threshold alert + LLM opinion". The LLM prompt text embeds contested directional priors (e.g. long liquidations = bottoms; the liquidation prompt calls this "shorts taking profit", which is not mechanically coherent) and was not tested. There is nothing here to replicate except the thresholds, which carry no evidence.
- The only genuinely testable ideas are: (a) one-sided liquidation burst -> same-direction continuation over ~5 minutes; (b) liquidation burst gated by a nearby public liquidation-price "pile of fuel" (not backtestable historically per the author); (c) HLP vault long/short positioning z-score and top-vs-bottom-100 trader positioning as contrarian/confirming signals; (d) fade of ATR-stretched 5-minute streaks. All are 5-minute-horizon BTC ideas expressed through Polymarket binaries, not perps.

- Contents of the paid GitHub repos ("Exclusive AI Agents GitHub", "My Trading Bot GitHub", `moon-dev-trading-bots`) are not public, so the "300+ bots" are not inspectable.

## Q2. Which strategies were backtested, with what metrics, periods, costs, out-of-sample checks; any verifiable live record?

### Takeaway
All RBI backtests in the readable repo run on one dataset: BTC-USD 15-minute bars from 2023-01-01 to 2023-11-20 (a strongly rising period, buy-and-hold +127.8%). In the only committed batch of raw execution outputs I could parse, 1 of 69 executed runs was profitable and none beat buy-and-hold. The headline "winners" in docs were tuned against Sharpe >= 2 / >100-trade targets on that same sample. There is no verifiable live track record for the strategy zoo; the nearest thing is on-chain Hyperliquid accounts in the AI Trading Battles experiment, which I could not verify and which is not a strategy edge claim.

### Cited Findings
**RBI backtest design and sample**
- Backtest prompt hard-codes one file: `BTC-USD-15m.csv`, first row 2023-01-01 00:00, last row 2023-11-20 19:15 (31,066 data rows; researcher-computed) with `Backtest(data, Strategy, cash=1_000_000, commission=0.002)` in the v3 parallel agent — [rbi_agent_pp_multi.py](https://github.com/brenwhyte/moon-dev-ai-agents/blob/main/src/agents/rbi_agent_pp_multi.py), [data file](https://github.com/yolojewjitsu/moon-dev-ai-agents/blob/main/src/data/rbi/BTC-USD-15m.csv). Commission 0.2% per side is a reasonable cost assumption for taker fees plus slippage, but no funding, no slippage model, single asset.
- Corpus size (researcher-computed from the mirror tree): 1,364 `.py` files in `backtests_final` and 1,349 in `backtests` folders across ~30 dated RBI runs, 13 Mar to 22 Apr 2025 plus 8 Aug 2025 and `rbi_v2/07_24_2025` — [tree](https://github.com/yolojewjitsu/moon-dev-ai-agents/tree/main/src/data). Only a minority have stored outputs.
- Raw execution results for the v2 batch (2025-07-24): 109 JSON files, 69 ran to completion with a stats block; researcher-computed: median return 0.0% (most had 0 trades), mean -1.05%, max +5.46% (106 trades, Sharpe 0.76), only 1 of 69 with positive return, 0 beat buy-and-hold of +127.77%, 0 with Sharpe > 1 — [execution_results](https://github.com/yolojewjitsu/moon-dev-ai-agents/tree/main/src/data/rbi_v2/07_24_2025/execution_results). Caveat: many are code-debug iterations of the same idea, so the 69 are not independent trials.
- A hand-written AI6 summary (generated 2025-07-14) reports three "divergence" strategies on the same BTC 15m file: DivergenceAnchor -9.28% (Sharpe -0.81, 17 trades), DivergenceBand +1.05% (Sharpe 0.21, 10 trades), DivergenceVolatility +2.61% (Sharpe 1.84, 125 trades), against $1M cash and 1% risk per trade — [AI6_SUMMARY_REPORT.md](https://github.com/yolojewjitsu/moon-dev-ai-agents/blob/main/src/data/rbi/03_14_2025/AI_BACKTEST_RESULTS/AI6_SUMMARY_REPORT.md). ### Inferences
- A 2023 BTC-only sample in an up-year with +128% buy-and-hold makes any long-biased strategy look good in-sample; the committed outputs show that even then the generated strategies mostly did not beat buy-and-hold. The "Sharpe 2.0 / 100 trades" targets were chosen first and strategies iterated until reported to meet them, so the reported values are in-sample maxima, not estimates.
- The Polymarket hit rates have small effective samples and post-hoc conditioning. Rough standard errors (researcher-computed, assuming independence): 58.8% on n=187 has 95% CI about 52-66%; 48.8% on n=41 about +/-15 points; 54.3% on n=8,802 is statistically distinguishable from 50% if windows were independent, but the 4-streak and 3x-ATR thresholds were chosen on the same 52-week set, and overlapping / serially dependent events inflate effective n. Payoff is a binary with fees and spread, so 54% at ~50c is ~4c gross (author's own number).
- The "95% continuation" statistic is likely close to mechanical: it conditions on a move that has already occurred partway through the 5-minute window, so continuation to the window end is largely guaranteed by partial-window path, unless measured from a fixed pre-signal time. I could not verify the exact construction; treat as unreliable until the entry-time alignment is checked.

### Gaps
- No out-of-sample, walk-forward or cost-stress results exist for the RBI zoo in anything I could read.
- Could not read the 12,000-bot or Karpathy-loop videos (transcripts not retrievable) to see what test and sample they used; "44,975%" appears only in a title.
- Could not verify any claim against on-chain Hyperliquid addresses (addresses are on the JS site, not in the repo README).

## Q4. Validation methods used and visible overfitting risks

### Takeaway
No Monte Carlo, walk-forward, out-of-sample, alpha-decay or multiple-testing control is implemented in the readable code; those terms appear only as aspirational bullet points in an LLM-generated report. The RBI pipeline selects on in-sample return against a fixed target, on one asset and one period, in bulk, then performs a post-hoc "multi-data test" in a non-public helper. This is a textbook backtest-overfitting setup. His 2026 Polymarket READMEs show better hygiene (logging skips, honest-risk sections, paper vs live separation, pre-defined kill switches) but their thresholds are still mined from the same logs.
