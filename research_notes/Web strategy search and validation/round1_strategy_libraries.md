# Round 1: candidate strategies from public strategy libraries (QuantConnect, Quantpedia and others)

Method note: about 16 tool calls. Only QuantConnect Strategy Library pages were fetched in full. Quantpedia Screener pages are paywalled and were not read. The Robot Wealth, Alvarez, Quantifiable Edges, Composer, TradingView and freqtrade searches found nothing usable: Alvarez results were mean reversion, the others returned nothing relevant. No backtests were run. "Reported performance: none" means the QC page shows none; the numbers live in attached backtests that were not visible. QC library pages are mostly written by QC staff or community members from Quantpedia summaries, and several run on discontinued Nasdaq Data Link datasets. The code is therefore the "published" spec, and the data must be substituted.

## Which library strategies can be coded exactly as published, with free data?

### Takeaway
Four candidates are usable as published, with data substitution flagged. Two more are conditional because the page leaves the universe or sizing unspecified. QC pages report almost no performance, so there is no published in-sample result to compare against, apart from the oil page (Sharpe 0.726 vs 0.735 benchmark, 2010-2017). Commodity term structure (futures chains) and sentiment/style rotation (fundamentals, put-call data) were rejected for data reasons.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Parameter-complete as published: C1 (once data swapped), C2, C3. C4 lacks its tickers and C5 lacks its universe; C6 lacks sizing. Under the 'skip without explicit parameters' rule, C4 to C6 are flagged conditional.
- C2 will likely be untestable because it will rarely fire in the free-data window, which is a power issue not a data issue. C1 has few independent decisions per year (12), so time-to-answer is slow.
- C3 requires a pre-registered choice on the code/text sign conflict. Registering both would double the trial count.
- Only C1 and C2 use a different information source (macro). C3-C5 are equity cross-sectional factors via ETFs; C6 is carry. None of the six has verifiable in-sample numbers except C1.

### Gaps
- Quantpedia Screener pages (Details/3, 5, 43, 78, 91) are paywalled; the original sample periods and performance figures were not obtained.
- Rejected, not run: Term Structure Effect in Commodities ([QC](https://www.quantconnect.com/learning/articles/investment-strategy-library/term-structure-effect-in-commodities)): needs futures chain data; the paper is Fuertes, Miffre and Rallis 2010; QC staff found no profit when tuned and profit appears to have concentrated in 1997-2007. Sentiment and Style Rotation ([QC](https://www.quantconnect.com/learning/articles/investment-strategy-library/sentiment-and-style-rotation-effect-in-stocks)): needs fundamentals for NYSE/NASDAQ stocks plus CBOE put-call and VIX; not free daily data and VIX is covered by other researchers.
- Quantpedia "Avoid Equity Bear Markets Part 3" ([article](https://quantpedia.com/avoid-equity-bear-markets-with-a-market-timing-strategy-part-3/)): yield curve (10y minus 3m > 0) plus a trend filter; the full spec depends on Part 1 definitions (Rachev ratio, INDPROD, RSALES, DIVIDEND), the held asset when out is unnamed, and YC alone added nothing. Reported 7.05% return, Sharpe 0.60 vs MKT 0.35, 1927-2022 (in-sample, hypothetical). Not selected because parameters are incomplete and it contains a 200-day SMA trend component.
- Pre-FOMC drift ([Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/explaining-fomc-drift/)): calendar effect and so a duplicate of other researchers; exact Quantpedia rules not visible.
- Earnings-announcement premium / SUE (listed in QC library): needs an earnings calendar; no free reliable source verified.
- Robot Wealth, Alvarez, Quantifiable Edges, Composer, TradingView and freqtrade: nothing usable and non-duplicate was found. Alvarez results were mean reversion ([TradingView script](https://in.tradingview.com/script/Cvi9Zf0q-ETF-3-Day-Reversion-Strategy)). A QC forum post describes a 13-ETF cross-asset momentum (average of 3, 6, 9, 12 month returns, top four, 30% short of worst) ([forum](https://www.quantconnect.com/forum/discussion/21050)); it is tactical allocation and was not used.
- Quantpedia's ETF momentum robustness article ([link](https://quantpedia.com/robustness-testing-of-country-and-asset-etf-momentum-strategies/)) was seen only as a search snippet; its findings were not read.
- No evidence was found of post-publication performance for C2-C6.
